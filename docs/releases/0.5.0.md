The fifth release of stafeto brings time budgets. The kernel gives a thread a sporadic-server budget of C per period T, `init` gives budgets to the network service and to the RAM file, pipe and terminal services, and the interface of the POSIX layer is frozen as the series 0.5. The kernel is still a small capability microkernel in Rust for AArch64; it boots in QEMU (`virt`) on any host, with HVF acceleration on a Mac with Apple silicon, under Apple Virtualization.framework, and on AWS EC2 Graviton.

## Try it

Download `stafeto-0.5.0-aarch64-virt-base.tar.xz` (the shell, the package tool and the base packages) or `stafeto-0.5.0-aarch64-virt-full.tar.xz` (every package, including the network programs; it needs 1024M of memory), unpack it and run `./run.sh` (`--cpus 4` for four CPUs, `--hvf` on a Mac with Apple silicon).

## Try it on AWS

0.5.0 has no AMI of its own. The public AMI stafeto-0.4.0 in eu-central-1 runs the 0.4.0 system; the next image for AWS comes with the try-out work (SSH, nginx, the package manager).

## Highlights

- **Time budgets in the kernel.** A thread can be bound to a sporadic-server budget of C per period T. Three new system calls, numbers 38 to 40, make them: `budget_create`, `thread_bind_budget` and `reply_receive`.
- **Budgets in `init`.** `init` gives budgets to the network service (`netd`) and to the RAM file, pipe and terminal services. Once its budget is spent, each of the three works at level 32. The guarantee: these three take at most C per T from the levels 33 and above. The levels at or below 32 get no share guarantee in 0.5.0; that is planned for 0.5.1.
- **Overrun journal.** `init` keeps a journal of the overruns of the budgets it hands out.
- **Preemption count.** `object_info(THREAD_STATE)` returns the number of times the kernel preempted the thread.
- **Scheduling interface.** `SCHED_SPORADIC` is defined and refused. The final C layouts are fixed: `struct sched_param` is 48 bytes, `pthread_attr_t` and `posix_spawnattr_t` are 96 bytes.
- **ABI series 0.5 frozen.** `packages/abi.txt` holds the fingerprint of the series: a package built for it runs on every system of the series.
- **Kernel size.** The size limit of the kernel image is lifted; `cargo xtask kernel-size` reports where the bytes come from.
- **Console and entropy.** Lines through the console service come out whole, with their headers. The entropy service keeps its seed across a restart of the service.
- **Probes on GitHub runners.** The probes of the tree run on GitHub Actions runners.

## Limits

- The levels at or below 32 get no share guarantee in 0.5.0; a service that works at level 1 once its budget is spent is planned for 0.5.1.
- The ENA driver uses one queue pair, and the Low Latency Queue mode is not implemented. Only the instance type `t4g.small` and the region `eu-central-1` are tested.
- Apple VZ gives the kernel no port: a kernel panic there shows nothing and powers the machine off.
- SMP runs on up to 8 CPUs (QEMU, HVF and VZ are tested with 4); the bounds of a real-time analysis on several CPUs still wait for a board.
- `fork` copies everything the layer maps, with no copy on write.
- While the kernel holds the port (an image with no console service), a line over 64 bytes goes out in pieces without their headers.
- After a restart of the entropy service the processes that ran before keep their generators but not their session: their children get `ENOSYS` from `getentropy`.
- The network is IPv4. `wget` checks no certificate and speaks TLS 1.2 only.
- The file system lives in RAM unless a disk is attached; changes are gone after a restart.
- Graphics is a preview with one full-screen program, and there is no sound yet.
- Nine POSIX utilities wait for services of their own: `at`, `batch`, `lp`, `mailx`, `locale`, `localedef`, `gettext`, `ngettext` and `msgfmt`.
- Interfaces may still change inside 0.x. The series 0.5 is frozen in `packages/abi.txt`: a package built for it runs on every system of the series.
