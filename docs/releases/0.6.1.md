The release 0.6.1 of stafeto is a patch release of the series 0.6. It is 0.6.0 with a faster repair of the data volume after a power cut, and nothing else changes. The kernel is still a small capability microkernel in Rust for AArch64; it boots in QEMU (`virt`) on any host, with HVF acceleration on a Mac with Apple silicon, under Apple Virtualization.framework, and on AWS EC2 Graviton.

## Try it

Download `stafeto-0.6.1-aarch64-virt-base.tar.xz` (the shell, the package tool and the base packages) or `stafeto-0.6.1-aarch64-virt-full.tar.xz` (every package, including the network programs; it needs 1024M of memory), unpack it and run `./run.sh` (`--cpus 4` for four CPUs, `--hvf` on a Mac with Apple silicon).

## Try it on AWS

The public AWS image of 0.6.1 replaces the image of 0.6.0. It has the same contents, launch steps and limits as described in the notes of 0.6.0 (`docs/releases/0.6.0.md`). Its AMI id is added to these notes when the image is published, and the image of 0.6.0 is closed then.

## What changed

The data service (`datafs`) mounts the data volume faster after a power cut that came inside an `rmdir` or inside a `rename` over a directory. Such a cut leaves an orphan: a directory that no entry names any more. The mount of 0.6.0 found orphans with about two walks of the whole list of metadata pairs for each directory. On a nearly full volume at the limits of the service (1,024 directories and 2,048 metadata pairs) the repair took 6,003,785 reads. The repair of 0.6.1 walks the list of pairs once and keeps what it needs in a table of 48 KiB in the memory of the service, and it takes 53,996 reads at the same limits.

In time, at the limits above:

- At 10,000 reads a second the mount took 600 s in 0.6.0 and takes 5.4 s in 0.6.1.
- On EBS gp3 at 3,000 reads a second it took about 33 minutes and takes 18 s.

A volume that was not cut, or was cut anywhere else, mounts as before. The result of the repair is the same as in 0.6.0: the same orphans are found and removed. A read error while the table is built sends the repair back to the walk of 0.6.0, and a full table answers for the pairs it holds and walks for the rest.

## Nothing else changes

The kernel, the services, the programs and the packages are the 0.6.0 ones. The only changed code is the data service and its copy of littlefs.

## Compatibility

The ABI of the series 0.6 stays frozen and does not change: `cargo xtask abi --check` reports `series 0.6 frozen, no change`, and the packages of 0.6.0 run on 0.6.1. The format of the data volume is the same as in 0.6.0: a volume of 0.6.0 mounts under 0.6.1 and the reverse. The limits of 0.6.0 apply to this release, and the data service takes 48 KiB more memory for the table of the repair.

## Third-party notices and sources

The notices of the system and of the packages of each image are in `LICENSES/` of the archives, as in 0.6.0. The third-party components are those of 0.6.0, and one has one more change:

- littlefs 2.11.3 (BSD-3-Clause) with the change of stafeto of 0.6.0 (three functions that read the user attribute of an entry and a fourth that counts metadata pairs) and one more: the table of the repair of orphans, in `lfs.c` and `lfs.h`. The change and its patch are in `third-party/littlefs/STAFETO-CHANGES.md` and `stafeto-changes.patch`, under the same licence.

The archive `stafeto-0.6.1-gpl-sources.tar.xz` holds the sources of the GPL packages of both images, the stafeto tree at the release commit and relibc at its pin. The tree is public at `https://github.com/stafeto/stafeto`.
